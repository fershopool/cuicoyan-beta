## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a lively, neo-Mexican aesthetic that celebrates the cultural density of Mexico City without relying on folkloric clichés, archaeological tropes, or nostalgic kitsch. Its character is contemporary, warm, structural, and deeply expressive—rooted in the sensory cadence of street theater, sones jarochos, huapangos, and urban arts spaces.

The target audience spans local cultural participants, independent artists, workshop attendees, and civic organizers navigating high-frequency schedules across diverse neighborhoods. The interface must evoke creative vitality, community solidarity, and decisive navigational ease. 

The stylistic approach combines **High-Contrast Editorial Boldness** with **Tactile Contemporary Card Layouts**. It leans into expressive, rounded geometric forms, warm structural paper tones (`Marfil` and `Papel Cempasúchil`), and a deliberate 70/20/10 proportional color rule:
- **70% Neutral Foundation:** Atmospheric canvas and container surfaces (`#FFF9F8` in light mode, `#080817` in dark mode).
- **20% Contextual Disciplines:** Clear semantic cues linking visual categories (Huapango, Fandango, Danza, Teatro, Música).
- **10% Brand Spark:** High-energy focal accents through accessible Bugambilia and dynamic multi-stop brand gradients.

## Colors

The color system operates on dual calibrated light and dark environments rather than algorithmic luminance inversions. Pure black (`#000000`) and pure clinical white (`#FFFFFF` on full-bleed backgrounds) are prohibited to maintain a tactile, organic visual warmth.

### Light Mode Harmony
- **Canvas Base (`#FFF9F8` - Marfil Cuicoyan):** The default root page background, establishing an artisanal paper warmth.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` & `#FFF3F6`):** Clean white is preserved strictly for cards, modal dialogs, and interactive controls, while `#FFF3F6` provides subtle rhythmic alternating bands.
- **Narrative Surface (`#FFF7EE` - Papel Cempasúchil):** Reserved exclusively for editorial blocks, community manifestos, and historical sidebars.
- **Primary Interactive (`#E91662` - Bugambilia Accesible):** Anchors calls-to-action, primary focus rings, and universal search active states.
- **Text (`#11152E` & `#5F6273`):** High-density blue-black for sharp typographic hierarchy, paired with deep lavender-gray secondary tones that satisfy WCAG 2.2 AA contrast ratios.

### Dark Mode Harmony
- **Canvas Base (`#080817` - Noche Cuicoyan):** A deep violet-tinted night sky preventing eye strain while keeping rich saturation.
- **Layered Surfaces (`#101126`, `#16132B`, `#1D1735`):** Progressive luminance layers replace drop shadows to create natural elevation.
- **High-Luminance Accents (`#FF3D8D`, `#17E0E4`, `#FF9D2E`, `#B45CFF`, `#24C8EF`, `#20D5A7`):** Specially shifted pastel-bright pigments optimized for legibility against dark substrates.

### Cultural Discipline Tokens
Disciplines maintain non-negotiable color identities across both modes:
- **Huapango:** `#009BA5` (Light) / `#17E0E4` (Dark)
- **Fandango:** `#E96F00` (Light) / `#FF9D2E` (Dark)
- **Danza:** `#7936D2` (Light) / `#B45CFF` (Dark)
- **Teatro:** `#D62650` (Light) / `#FF506F` (Dark)
- **Música:** `#008EB5` (Light) / `#24C8EF` (Dark)
- **Talleres / Comunidades:** `#E91662` (Light) / `#FF3D8D` (Dark)

## Typography

The typographic voice pairs the rhythmic, humanistic quirk of **Bricolage Grotesque** with the utilitarian precision of **Manrope**.
- **Display & Headings (Bricolage Grotesque):** Expressive characters, energetic terminals, and punchy visual tension. Used for hero impact, editorial statements, venue headers, and card titles. Weights below `600` are forbidden to maintain bold graphic authority.
- **Interface, Prose & Metadata (Manrope):** Clean geometry, balanced vertical metrics, and crisp numerals. Designed for high legibility across dense programming grids, dates, ticket pricing, and multi-paragraph event descriptions.
- **Eyebrows & Discipline Labels:** Always set uppercase in Manrope `700` with `+0.08em` tracking to provide crisp architectural anchors above expressive editorial headlines.
- **Constraints:** Line lengths for editorial prose must cap at 65–72 characters per line. Hero copy must never exceed 12 words. Novelty faux-prehispanic typefaces are prohibited.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a **strict 4px base rhythm** set inside a responsive fluid container model capped at **1280px max-width**.

### Spatial Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1440px Canvas):** 12-column grid with `5rem` (80px) outer margins, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and generous vertical rhythm between sections (`7rem` to `9rem`).
- **Tablet (834px Canvas):** 8-column grid with `2.5rem` (40px) outer margins and `1.25rem` (20px) gutters. Content blocks split symmetrically before reflowing to single columns when individual block widths drop below 320px.
- **Mobile (390px Canvas):** 4-column grid with `1.25rem` (20px) safe-area padding and `1rem` (16px) gutters. Carousels utilize a right-side peek offset of `16px` to signal horizontal overflow.

### Editorial & Form Containers
- Full-bleed layouts are restricted to the Hero section, the cultural purpose band, and the final CTA.
- Long-form narrative and manifesto blocks are strictly constrained between `720px` and `760px` centered to guarantee comfortable ocular tracking.
- Form containers and checkout steps are constrained between `560px` and `640px`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **tactile structural planes and tonal layering** rather than generic, heavy drop shadows.

### Light Mode: Tactile Warmth
- **Base Surfaces:** Rest flat on `#FFF9F8` with delicate hairline borders (`1px solid #ECE4E7`).
- **Floating Cards & Modals:** Elevated with an ambient, diffused violet-tinted shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(17, 21, 46, 0.08)`.
- **Active / Hover State:** Cards lift physically by `translateY(-3px)` with an expanded shadow `0 14px 36px rgba(17, 21, 46, 0.12)`.

### Dark Mode: Tonal Stepping
- Drop shadows are removed in favor of structural background shift:
  1. Base canvas: `#080817`
  2. Standard cards and panels: `#101126` (framed with `1px solid #292540`)
  3. Intermediate sections & flyouts: `#16132B`
  4. Elevated overlays & active cards: `#1D1735` (framed with active border `#3A3154`)
- **Focused Accents:** Active category pills and search inputs receive a localized, low-spread luminescent aura: `0 0 24px rgba(255, 61, 141, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

The geometric identity is defined by friendly, high-radius curves (`16px` to `24px`) that recall contemporary industrial design and organic botanical curves, balanced against disciplined structural grid layouts.

- **Micro Controls & Date Badges (`12px`):** Used for calendar day indicators, timestamp tags, and compact icon buttons.
- **Interactive Standard (`16px`):** Buttons, search inputs, textareas, and select pickers.
- **Core Cards (`20px – 24px`):** Event cards, artist profiles, venue listings, and map popovers.
- **Editorial Containers (`24px – 32px`):** Major manifesto panels, category spotlights, and hero presentation blocks.
- **Capsule / Pill (`999px`):** Filter tags, category selectors, notification counter badges, and the sticky global search capsule.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#E91662` (Light) / `#FF3D8D` (Dark) with white/dark-night high-contrast label in Manrope 600. Height 48px desktop, 52px mobile. Corner radius `16px`. On hover, scale to `1.02` with brief transition (180ms ease-out). On active, compress to `0.98`.
- **Secondary Button:** Surface fill with a `1.5px solid` hairline border (`#ECE4E7` / `#292540`). Hover state transitions to soft tinted fill (`#FFF3F6` / `#16132B`).
- **Signature Gradient Button (Hero & Final CTA):** Utilizes the dynamic linear gradient (`#F21F6D` → `#8A3FFC` → `#00C7D4`) at a 135-degree angle. Restricted to no more than one primary instance per viewport.

### Category Chips & Filters
- Compact pill-shaped elements (`999px` radius) with minimum touch targets of 44px.
- Enforces the triple token pattern: **Icon + Discipline Label + Specific Accent Color**.
- Unselected chips feature a neutral outline (`#ECE4E7` / `#292540`) with text in `#5F6273`. Selected chips fill with a 12% tint of their respective discipline color, an accentuated `1.5px` border matching the discipline token, and bold text.

### Cards (Events, Venues, Artists)
- Built on `20px` to `24px` rounded surfaces. 
- Media frames maintain a strict `4:3` (events) or `1:1` (artists) ratio with top corners clipped to match card curvature.
- Cards feature an upper eyebrow with the discipline badge and date token, followed by a Bricolage Grotesque card title (`22px`), and logistical metadata (neighborhood, venue name, ticket cost) set in Manrope body-sm.
- Elevation shifts by `-3px` on pointer hover with smooth 200ms cubic-bezier transition.

### Input Fields & Global Search
- Height 52px to 56px with a `16px` corner radius.
- Background uses pure white `#FFFFFF` (Light) or `#101126` (Dark) with a `1px` structural outline (`#ECE4E7` / `#292540`).
- Focus state reveals a `2px` crisp border ring in Bugambilia (`#E91662`) or high-luminance Turquesa (`#17E0E4` in dark mode) with a 2px spatial offset.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Custom rounded boxes (`6px` for checkbox, `50%` for radio) with `20px` bounding box inside a `44px` hit area.
- Unchecked state matches structural border `#ECE4E7` / `#292540`. Checked state smoothly fills with `#E91662` (or dark mode `#FF3D8D`) and displays a crisp white checkmark or center pip.

### Lists & Cultural Agendas
- Alternating agenda rows separated by hairline dividers (`#ECE4E7` / `#292540`).
- Every territorial or map-based display must provide a symmetrical, high-contrast accessible list view toggle for full screen-reader and mobility compliance.
